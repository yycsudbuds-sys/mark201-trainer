**Positional-reference scan:** no explanation refers to an option by letter or position. Phrases such as "the other pairings", "the reversed version is the trap", "the 'before launch' option" and "every option that includes 'demographic'" point to content, not position, so they still work when options are shuffled. I left them alone, apart from q06-113 above.

**Focus areas:** all checked and correct.
- **Variable families:** keepers, competitive achievers and adventurous risk-takers are psychographic. Benefits sought, user status, loyalty, occasion and usage rate are behaviouristic. Generation, family life cycle, income and household size are demographic.
- **Head-to-head vs differentiation positioning:** q06-116 to q06-121 and q06-018 are correct.
- **Forming vs selecting criteria:** q06-036 to q06-047 are correct, including the "cost of assigning" (forming) vs "cost of reaching" (selecting) trap.
- **POP vs POD:** q06-137 to q06-141 are correct.
- **Calculations:** q06-035 is -$35,000, so do not segment. q06-074 is 20% of customers, giving 0.8 x $500,000 = $400,000.

- **q06-050 and q06-142:** the answers ("Operating characteristics" and "unique selling proposition (USP)") are somewhat longer than the distractors only because the terms themselves are longer. This is not a clear giveaway.
