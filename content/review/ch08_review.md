- **q08-053** (Swiffer Sweeper high-learning vs WetJet continuous innovation): the "how can both be valid" premise is a little loose because these are two different products. The keyed answer (two different frameworks) is still the only defensible one.

In q08-094, "product improvement" is not an option. The concept card states the rule (upgrade plus higher price = trading up).
- **q08-037 and the c08-plc-extension card** (extension strategies are used mainly in maturity, or late growth and maturity): the inventory does not state this. It agrees with the inventory's maturity description (line extensions, holding share).
- **q08-136** (diversification is the riskiest growth strategy): not stated in the inventory, but it follows from new product + new market and is standard.
- **c08-adoption-barriers definitions** (usage = clashes with existing habits; value = no incentive to change; risk = physical, economic or social; psychological = cultural or image): the inventory lists only the four names. These definitions are the standard textbook ones and nothing in the inventory contradicts them.
