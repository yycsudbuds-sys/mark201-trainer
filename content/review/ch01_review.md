## Borderline, left alone

- **q01-012 (stem) and q01-062 / q01-070 (explanations)** use content ordinals: "second requirement", "third step" and "third element". These refer to the inventory's numbered lists, not to option positions. q01-012 also quotes the requirement in parentheses. Kept.
- **"The other three statements/are..." in q01-005, q01-013 and q01-061.** These do not depend on position (they mean "every option except the key"), and q01-013 then names each one. Kept.
- **QA flagged the keyed answer as much longer than the distractors in q01-053, q01-069, q01-094 and q01-106.** q01-053 is official, so it cannot be changed. In the other three the options are bare term names ("Relationship/societal orientation", "Customer experience management", "Possession utility"). The extra length comes from the term itself, so it is not a content giveaway. Not a clear case.
- **q01-059** (non-profit, what comes next): one could argue that step 2, managing the mix, is ongoing. But the stem says the workshops "has launched" and are already promoted, so step 3 (realize objectives) is the only fit.
- **q01-074** (TF, "firms that put great effort into implementing the marketing concept are said to have a market orientation"): this is not stated word for word in the inventory. The official explanation for practice question 6 supports it ("putting huge effort into implementing the marketing concept ... market orientation"). Kept as True.
- **q01-044** ("bundle free ski tuning"): this could be read as a product or a promotion decision. Either way it is a controllable 4 P decision, which is all the item tests.
- **q01-087** keeps "none of the above" because it is an official question. It is the chapter's only all/none-of-the-above item.
