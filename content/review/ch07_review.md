# Chapter 7 review — Products and Brands

I checked every question's keyed answer and every concept card against `kb/inventory_ch07.md`. I also checked the 6 activities and compared the 10 official questions with `kb/Extra_Chapter_7_Questions.txt`. All 156 keyed answers are correct. Each one has a single defensible answer, and the stems carry the cue needed to pick it.

I paid special attention to these areas:
- **Product layers.** Every warranty, repair, delivery, installation, FAQ, video, support line and guarantee is keyed augmented. Every feature, design, brand name, packaging and quality level is keyed actual. Every fundamental benefit is keyed core. Packaging is treated as actual throughout (q07-016, q07-136).
- **Consumer product classes.** Convenience, shopping, specialty and unsought scenarios are keyed on the buyer's behaviour, and the "depends on the individual" questions (q07-027, q07-032, q07-038) are consistent.
- **The four I's.** In every scenario the cue points to one I only: variation in quality is inconsistency, and the provider being the service, or simultaneous delivery, is inseparability.
- **Branding strategies.** Brand extension (same name, new class), sub-branding (parent name plus a new name), co-branding (two manufacturers), licensing (royalty or fee), mixed branding and multibranding are kept distinct. A new scent or size is correctly called deepening the line, not a brand extension.
- **Legal protection.** Trademark, copyright and patent are matched correctly, and copyright duration is lifetime + 50 years throughout.
- **Item, line and mix.** Product item/SKU, line depth and length, and mix width follow the inventory's definitions. The q07-063 calculation checks out: 3 lines + 1 = 4 lines, and 18 is a count of items.

## Borderline, left alone

- **c07-inconsistency definition** adds "as well as when and where it is delivered" to the inventory's "quality varies with the people who deliver it". This is a standard textbook broadening, it contradicts nothing, and every inconsistency question is keyed on people-driven variation.
- **Line length = "total number of items"** (q07-060, c07-product-line). This follows the inventory. Some textbooks define depth and length differently, so I made sure no question offers both "depth" and "length" for the same count. q07-057 offers depth only.
- **q07-022 / c07-augmented-product** ("augmented features distinguish the product from competitive offerings"). The inventory does not state this, but the official answer explanations for the Spectra and Patagonia questions use the same wording.
- **q07-114** (multibranding lets each brand target its own segment without diluting the other's image). This rationale is not in the inventory, but it is standard, and no other option is defensible.
- **q07-150 and the four-I's figure** ("focus on the people and the service encounter" as the response to inseparability). The inventory gives no explicit response for inseparability. This one follows from its definition.
- **q07-051** keeps "Primary demand" as a distractor. The stem is about a supplier's orders rising because consumers bought more of the final product, which is derived demand only.
- **Length flags I left alone** (q07-071, q07-090, q07-107, q07-111, q07-152 and a few others at 1.3–1.4×). The extra length comes from naturally longer terms or parentheticals, such as "Brand associations (brand image)" and "manufacturer (national)", not from a padded answer.
- **Official q07-019, q07-050, q07-055 and q07-076** keep their 5 options, including "none of the above" or "all of the above", because they match the instructor's originals.
